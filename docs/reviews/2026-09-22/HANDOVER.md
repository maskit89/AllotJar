## Important corrections and constraints

- Plans never create money. Actual account money and its allocation to jars must reconcile; do not just stop summing plans without implementing actual funding and opening allocations.
- Each jar has one designated savings account. A negative jar is allowed; a negative savings account is not. Same-account reallocations do not change account balances.
- Cross-account reallocation records a transfer after explicit **“I've completed this transfer”** confirmation. Both statement sides must later match it without another balance effect.
- Each transfer leg uses its bank's posting date. Between departure and arrival the money is shown in transit and belongs to neither jar (rule 69); combined account balances plus transit must conserve the amount.
- Positive jar balances must not exceed their savings account's actual money (rule 70). Negative jars cannot conceal insufficient backing; require an explicit user-selected reallocation to cover the deficit.
- Each budget uses one currency. Bank/user amounts are authoritative. Conversion suggestions are allowed only where the required amount is missing and require approval before application. Do not implement the earlier blanket ban on suggestion calculations, automatic conversion/reconciliation, or inferred fees.
- Private accounts cannot back shared jars. The earlier partially hidden/shared-account suggestion was explicitly rejected.
- Opening balance is at the **start** of its date. Historical corrections use actual transaction dates. Historical plans are not rewritten by those corrections.
- Completion, deadline rollover, acknowledgement, archiving and moving money are separate operations. Status transitions do not invent payment transactions.
- The category note is deferred because its author cannot recall the intended meaning. Do not silently turn it into a new category design.
- No production-conversion remediation workflow is needed. Rule 65 explicitly authorises starting with a fresh project development database when the new schema is ready. Rule 66 authorises individual PRs and sequential production releases for the agreed work.

## Decisions taken on 26 September 2026 (money movement slice)

No new rule was needed; rules 74, 78, 79 and 80 were implemented as written. Where the rules left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P6 Transfers out of a jar-backed account.** Rule 79 names the budget line as the way a payment identifies its jar; a transfer has no line. The transfer form (and an imported debit marked as a transfer) offers **From jar**, defaulting to unallocated, so jar money can leave through an ordinary transfer without a "Move money" detour. Example: Savings 1,000 (Holiday 400); a 200 transfer to Current from unallocated leaves Holiday at 400; choosing Holiday leaves it at 200.
- **P7 Edits re-answer the prompts.** Editing a transaction on a jar-backed account shows the rule 78/79 prompt again, pre-filled with the stored answer, and the edit sends the current answer; an edit that omits it returns the leg to its default (unallocated, or the line's jar). Deleting a transaction removes its answer with it.
- **P8 Held import rows are not persisted.** Rule 11 leaves batch handling open. The commit imports the rows that fit and returns the held ones with their reason; the review table keeps only those, the user records the missing deposit and commits them again, or unticks to discard. Leaving the page loses the held rows; re-uploading the statement offers only the rows not yet imported (dedup).
- **P9 Cross-account Move money is a completed transfer.** The modal asks for "I have made this transfer", the departure date (default today) and the arrival date (default today, clearable). Cleared, the amount is in transit and the destination jar receives it when the receipt is confirmed on the Transactions page (rule 71); the stored answer applies at that moment.
- **P10 Rule 74 is a whole-history check.** The balance must be at or above zero on every date: a back-dated payment that dips a past balance is refused even when today's balance would stay positive; shrinking, moving or deleting an income that later payments relied on is refused; lowering an opening balance below what recorded payments need is refused. Refusals state the account, the balance it would reach and the date.
- **P11 Import commit takes no ledger lock.** The transaction, transfer, delete, leg-confirmation, allocation and account-edit paths take the per-account lock so racing writers validate against committed money; the import commit keeps its existing collision-retry save without the lock, so a manual payment recorded during a commit is the one race not serialised. Recorded as a known limit, not silently accepted.

## Decisions taken on 27 September 2026 (W11 pocket money)

Rules 53 and 54 were implemented as written (the user said "let's proceed" on W11 without further questions). Where the rules left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P13 "Cancel" drops the plan to what was paid.** Rule 54 forbids cancelling from altering actual balances or removing recorded payments. A cancelled pocket-money line therefore keeps every payment recorded against it and its planned amount falls to the paid amount (never below; an overspent line is left as it is). A line nothing was ever recorded against is removed. "Keep" leaves the current month exactly as planned; later months are cancelled either way.
- **P14 "Current month" is the current UTC calendar month.** The keep-or-cancel question concerns the month the archive happens in, on the same UTC basis as every other "today" in the API — not the month selected in the top bar. Months before it are history and are never touched.
- **P15 A rename follows only the lines that carry the person's name.** Renaming a person renames their linked pocket-money lines in every month, but a line the user gave a name of their own keeps it; amounts and totals never move with a rename.
- **P16 Existing unlinked lines are adopted by name, once.** A "Pocket Money" line created before this change has no link. The next sync links the line whose name matches the person (case-insensitive) instead of adding a second one; from then on the id is the identity and the name is free to change. This is the only name-based matching left, and it applies only to lines with no link.
- **P17 Restoring a person recreates nothing.** Un-archiving (API-only, PPL-077) does not bring back cancelled lines; the Allocation page's "Add pocket money" sync creates the person's line for the month again when it is run. Copying a month into a new one drops an archived person's pocket-money line along with archived jars' lines.

## Decisions taken on 27 September 2026 (W12 reports and refunds)

Rules 37–39 and 41 were implemented as written (the user said "Start W12"; the only stop agreed in advance was O9's refund edges, which stay open above). Where the rules left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P18 A refund is money received, linked to its purchase.** A refund is an Income-typed transaction carrying the id of the purchase it refunds (`RefundOfTransactionId`), so it credits its account on the date it arrived and the register, imports and account balances need no new movement kind. It is left out of every income figure (received income, Variance, People income, the Money-in hero) and can sit only on an expense or savings line, where it reduces that line's spending (rule 38) in the month the line belongs to (rule 39). Both rows stay; nothing is netted away.
- **P19 The default line continues the purchase's line in the refund's month.** A refund with no category chosen goes to the purchase's own line when the refund falls in the same budget month; otherwise to the same jar's line, else to the line with the same group kind, group name and line name in the refund's month. When none exists — the purchase was uncategorised, the month has no such line, or the jar line's account is not the account refunded — the refund stays uncategorised and reduces the Uncategorised row. The payee's learned income line is never used for a refund.
- **P20 The pair stays coherent.** Refunds of one purchase never add up to more than was paid (partial refunds are allowed and accumulate); a refund can't be dated before its purchase; only an owned expense (not a transfer, not a refund) can be refunded; a refund can't be split; a purchase with refunds can't be deleted until they are unlinked or deleted; edits keep the types locked (a refund stays money received, a refunded purchase stays a payment), the purchase's amount at or above what was refunded and the dates in order. "Not a refund" turns the row back into plain income and takes it off the expense line.
- **P21 Reports add a transaction-dated layer to the line-derived figures.** Categorised spending keeps the line's budget month (the existing derivation); uncategorised transactions and unplaced refunds are reported by their own date (rule 39). Rows awaiting an exchange rate are left out and counted, as on the Budget page. A month with no budget still reports its transactions in the annual table (received, spent, uncategorised) with dashes where a budget would be. Received income includes uncategorised income and excludes transfers and refunds (rule 41).
- **P22 The annual table reads Budgeted · Received · Variance · Spent · Net.** Variance = received − budgeted, signed (`+€200.00` / `-€200.00`, plain at zero, never worded as a shortfall); Net = received − spent. REVIEW concern 5 (the annual table mixing budgeted income against actual spend) is closed by this.
- **P23 A group that nets below zero stays on the breakdown.** A category whose refunds exceed its spending in the month is kept, sorted last and labelled "after €X refunded" with no share; shares are of positive spending only. This replaces the earlier behaviour of dropping the row (RPT-023 rewritten).

## Decisions taken on 27 September 2026 (W02 schedules and previews)

The user answered the six questions put before W02 ("1. Yes 2. Yes 3. Yes but it can be archived 4. … one preselected currency … 5. Yes 6. YES!"), recorded as rules 82–86 and in O2/O3/O5/O6/O8 above. Rules 12–18, 35–36, 58–59 and 62 were then implemented as written, with rule 46 (W04) alongside because the replant and the preview share the planner. Where the rules left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P24 Exact cents, remainder last.** The remaining amount is divided over the contribution months floored to the cent, and the rounding remainder goes into the final month (rule 35: 100 over three months is 33.33, 33.33, 33.34; 1,000 over six is 166.66 ×5 then 166.70). A deadline in the current month, or already passed, plans the whole remainder now — one month, never zero. A negative month is never planned.
- **P25 The deadline day is never a contribution day; the deadline month is an opt-in only when the deadline is not the 1st.** Funding completes at the end of the month before the deadline (rule 12). "Include the deadline month" (rule 36) is offered, stored and honoured only when the deadline falls after the 1st; a 1st-of-month deadline has no such option because the month before is the last chance for the money to arrive. Dates are plain calendar dates (rule 82).
- **P26 This month's figures come from the month's start.** The plan shown for the current month is the saved Savings line when the month's budget exists, else the schedule from the jar's balance entering the month (the ledger as of the last day of the previous month); contributed = the balance's growth since the month began, floored at zero; to go = plan − contributed, floored at zero (rule 14). The planning month counts as a contribution month until it ends (rule 82).
- **P27 Later months are projected through the saved plans.** Seeding a month after the current one starts from the jar's balance entering the current month and folds in the saved planned lines of the months in between (or the schedule's own figure where a month has no budget), so what is seeded matches what the preview lists. A month at or before the current one plans from the ledger balance at its start.
- **P28 The preview is the planner.** `POST /jars/schedule` runs the same `JarPlanner` that seeds and replants Savings lines, for a new jar (from what is already set aside) or an edited one (from its actual balance); the client computes no schedule of its own. Per month the answer carries whether a budget exists and its shortfall (rule 59: total planned − the jar's current line + the new contribution − income, floored at zero), which the form lists in full and flags without trimming the plan.
- **P29 A schedule edit replants the current and later months.** Saving a change to kind, target, deadline, method, monthly amount, deadline-month option or cycle on an active jar rewrites the planned amount of its line in the current and every later existing month, appending the line where a later month lacks it (rule 46); a rename or account change touches no line; past months, transactions and balances are never touched. The confirmation rule 46 asks for is the preview in the same form before Save. A hand-edited line in a later month is overwritten by this — protecting manual overrides stays in O3.
- **P30 No plan means no contribution, not ÷12.** A new or edited jar must have a deadline or a monthly amount (rule 17): the client and the server validators refuse otherwise. A legacy jar that still has neither plans 0, reads **Needs a plan**, and is copied flat by a month copy. A funded jar (balance at or above its target) reads **Funded** even when it has no plan, and its excess above target is shown (rule 47).
- **P31 Retired modes migrate to the deadline method.** `JarSchedules` sets `Accrual = TargetByDate` on every jar not already there and drops `CoverStart`/`CoverEnd`; `MonthlyContribution` and `IncludeDeadlineMonth` are added. A jar saving a fixed amount each month is `Accrual = FixedMonthly` with a null deadline; a recurring jar is always by deadline. The DTO's `RequiredMonthlyContribution`/`ProjectedFullyFundedDate` are replaced by `PlannedThisMonth`/`ContributedThisMonth`/`RemainingThisMonth`, `Deadline`, `FundingCompletesOn`, `EstimatedMonthsToTarget` and the statuses `Ongoing`/`Unscheduled` (the old `Unfunded` is gone).

## Decisions taken on 27 September 2026 (W03 recurring lifecycle)

Rules 19–24, 57 and 83 were implemented as written. Where they left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P32 The open cycle is persisted and settled lazily.** A Recurring jar stores the deadline of its open cycle (`Jar.CycleDeadline`); the anchor `TargetDate` still fixes the day of month and the next cycle is always stepped from it, so a 31st clamps only in shorter months and never drifts (rule 62). Nothing runs on a timer: whenever jars are read, previewed, edited or a month is created, every open cycle whose deadline has passed is settled — closed as **Funded** and advanced when the jar held its target at the end of the day before the deadline (rule 21; money arriving on the deadline day is not counted, rule 82), left open otherwise. Several cycles passed unseen settle in order.
- **P33 Four outcomes, each with a balance snapshot.** A closed cycle records its deadline, the target it worked toward, the balance at the end of the day before the deadline (when the deadline had passed), and one of **Paid** (marked paid with a payment; on time when the payment is dated before the deadline), **Funded** (rolled forward held in full, no payment recorded), **Missed** or **Funded late** (dismissed short, or dismissed after reaching the target late). A closed cycle is never recomputed: a later ledger correction changes the jar's balance and any open cycle, never a recorded outcome (O2).
- **P34 The jar's own payments first, every expense on request, one cycle per payment.** Mark paid lists the expenses drawn from the jar (the jar's Savings line in its account, rule 79, or a stored allocation) that no cycle has claimed yet, newest first; "Show payments from other accounts" widens the list to every expense, for a bill paid elsewhere. Income, transfers and refunds are never offered or accepted; a future-dated payment is refused; a payment fulfils at most one cycle (unique index). Marking the open cycle paid closes it as Paid and advances the jar; a cycle that already rolled forward Funded can be linked to its payment afterwards from the card without touching the open cycle. Deleting the transaction later clears the link but keeps the recorded date and amount.
- **P35 A passed open cycle plans its whole shortfall now.** While a cycle is missed the month's plan is the full shortfall (the retired silent roll spread it over the next cycle), so the Budget page and the card agree with the rose "€150.00 short". Nothing is rolled, nothing is moved.
- **P36 Dismissing records what the balance says today.** Dismissing the notice closes the open cycle as **Funded late** when the jar holds its target today, else **Missed**, and the jar saves toward its next deadline from whatever it holds (rule 19); the request carries the cycle's deadline so a stale page cannot dismiss a cycle that has since changed. A cycle whose deadline has not passed cannot be dismissed.
- **P37 The planning balance is `min(entering the month, now)` — amends P26/P27.** Money leaving the jar this month (the bill paid from it) counts at once, so the plan after a payment is the real remainder (rule 23: 600 saved, 550 paid, 50 carried, 550 to save); a contribution this month still never shrinks the month's plan (rule 14). Later months are projected from the month's expected end (balance now plus what the plan still expects this month).
- **P38 Echoing the open cycle's date keeps the cycle.** The form shows a Recurring jar's open cycle's date as "Next due"; saving it back unchanged (or the stored anchor) keeps both the anchor and the cycle, so a rename never resets a cycle that has advanced; any other date is a deliberate change and becomes the new anchor and the new open cycle. A Goal has no cycle; switching a Recurring jar to a Goal keeps the date the user saw as the Goal's deadline.
- **P39 Legacy jars start on the cycle they were showing.** The `JarCycles` migration sets each existing Recurring jar's open cycle to the deadline the retired silent roll displayed (whole cycles from the anchor, then one more step while still in the past), so no jar wakes up Deadline missed for a cycle it never showed; Deadline missed applies from the first deadline after the deploy.

## Decisions taken on 27 September 2026 (W04 edits, deletion, archive, completion, skip)

Rules 25–27, 55–56, 60–61 and 84 were implemented as written (rule 46 had come with W02, rule 47's excess shows on the card). Where they left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P40 Stopping a jar cancels its plan from this month on, and only its plan.** Archiving (rule 25) or completing (rule 55) a jar cuts the current month's line to what has actually gone into the jar since the month began (so rule 14's "in" and "to go" stay coherent and nothing more is asked for) and removes each later month's line, which was a plan only — unless a transaction has been assigned to that line, in which case the line stays at 0 so the transaction keeps its place. Past months, transactions and balances are never touched; no money moves. Restoring an archived jar replants the current and later months from its schedule (rule 26's zero-balance requirement for archiving is unchanged).
- **P41 Completing is a Goal's ending; an overdrawn or unreadable balance refuses it.** Only a Goal can be marked completed (a recurring jar's cycle is marked paid, or the jar archived); a completed Goal keeps its money until the user moves or spends it, stays listed with a **Completed** chip and "Completed · €X left to move", and cannot be completed twice or turned into a recurring jar. A Goal whose balance is negative (rule 56's "positive remainder" cannot be applied) or unavailable (rule 67) is refused with the reason; covering the shortfall first is the way through until O3 answers negative-balance completion. Nothing un-completes a Goal (reopening stays in O3).
- **P42 Deletion eligibility is "never held money", read from the ledger.** `DELETE /jars/{id}` succeeds only when no allocation posting has ever touched the jar and no transaction has ever been assigned to one of its lines (rule 84); it removes the jar, every planned line it had in any month and its cycle history. Any other jar is refused with a pointer to Archive. The Edit form offers **Delete jar** (two clicks, like Archive) only when the server says the jar can be deleted.
- **P43 A jar's line cannot be deleted from a month; the ✕ asks skip or archive.** `DELETE /budget/items/{id}` refuses a jar's contribution line (the jar's plan would only put it back). On the Budget page the line's ✕ opens a dialog that explains this and offers **Skip this month** or **Archive the jar** (two clicks; rule 26's refusal shown verbatim), with the server's preview of what skipping does to the later months.
- **P44 A skipped month is a flagged line at 0, and the flag protects it.** Skipping sets the line's planned amount to 0 and marks it `IsSkipped`; every replant (a schedule edit, a cycle closing, a restore, a skip or un-skip) reads the flagged months and plans 0 there while the other months carry the remaining amount split exactly as rule 35 says (rule 61: €300 by December with September skipped → October €150, November €150). Month creation never recreates a skipped contribution because the line still exists. **Plan it again** clears the flag and replants; typing an amount on a skipped line also clears the flag (a typed amount is a plan). The Jars card reads "Skipped this month" and the form's preview lists the skipped months as such.
- **P45 The last eligible month can be skipped; the preview says what stays unplanned.** Skipping leaves no month to spread the remainder over when the deadline is next month: the planner never invents a month after the deadline (rule 36), so the remainder is reported as **unplanned** in the dialog ("€300.00 unplanned: no month is left to spread it over before the deadline") and the user decides. The same figure shows in the Edit form's preview while the skip stands.
- **P46 A past month is history.** Skipping or un-skipping a line in a month that is over is refused, and the Budget page shows neither the ✕ nor "Plan it again" on a past month's jar lines (rule 46/60: nothing rewrites history).

## Decisions taken on 28 September 2026 (W05 period consistency and refresh)

Rules 40, 48 and 49 were implemented as written (rule 40's opening-date cutoff and rule 49's dated corrections were already the ledger's behaviour and are now pinned by tests; rule 48 already governed the Budget page's Available column). Where the rules left an implementation choice, the following proposals were applied and stand unless the user objects (each is a small, reversible change):

- **P47 The Budget page's jars shelf is the viewed month's view of the jars.** `GET /jars?year=&month=` (and `JarFigures` behind every jar read) takes the budget month the figures belong to: a past month's balances are read as of its last day (rule 48's example: 300 at September's end while today's balance is 400), its plan is that month's own Savings line, "in" is what went into the jar during that month and "to go" what was still short of the plan at its end; the current month reads as before; a month that has not begun reads today's balances with nothing contributed yet and its own line as the plan — the future is never read. A month that is over and has no line for the jar (created later, or never planted there) plans nothing for it after the fact (rule 46); a month that has not begun and has no line yet is planned from that month, from today's balance. A month that is over also lists a jar archived since, when it carries the jar's line, because the grid beside it still shows that line. The shelf says which ("€300.00 saved as of 30 Sep 2026"; "balances as of today" on a later month) and shows nothing extra on the current month. The shelf and the Savings lines' Available figure now read one ledger at one date, so they agree on every month (REVIEW J6's second half; DASH-030's documented difference is gone).
- **P48 The Jars page always reads today.** It is where money is moved, cycles are marked paid and jars are completed, so its cards, hero and unallocated figures are today's whatever month the top bar shows; history is read on the Budget page. A month-scoped Jars page would need every action gated to the current month and is not asked for by any rule.
- **P49 Looking ahead settles nothing, and history keeps its status.** Cycles are settled by today's date, never by the month being viewed, so a Recurring jar whose deadline falls in a later month stays open until the deadline arrives. A past month's status is what it was at that month's end (Funded then, Behind then, Overspent then), and a Goal's completion counts from the day it was marked (rule 55), so a month before it reads the status, deadline and horizon the jar had then; a Recurring jar's past month is judged against the cycle that was still open at that month's end (its deadline and target, read from the closed cycle's record), so a missed September stays **Deadline missed** on September after the user marks the cycle paid in October, and its history line shows only the cycles closed by then; a jar's balance on a past date is what its allocations held in whichever account it was designated to then (rule 80 keeps that history in the old account's ledger; a month before the jar's current account opened still reads what an earlier account held, and only a month before any account held it is unavailable). A past month's funded-by and months-to-target figures are the jar's current ones, which the shelf does not show.
- **P50 What re-reads the shelf.** Changing the viewed month, editing a jar line's planned amount (it is the month's plan, rule 14, and it un-skips the month), creating the month (the seeded lines are its plan), skipping a month or planning it again, archiving a jar from its line and creating a jar each re-read `/jars` for the month. Every page reads afresh on load, so a transaction recorded on another page shows on the next visit; no cross-page push is added.
